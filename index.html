Our Brand

Luxury is never loud.

Fine Grain Millwork is a custom millwork house dedicated to exceptional craftsmanship, premium materials, and timeless design. Based in Barrie, Ontario, we build kitchens and cabinetry that are meant to be lived with for generations, not renovated again in ten years.

We believe true luxury lives in the details — meticulously crafted, thoughtfully designed, and built to last. From concept to installation, we deliver millwork that elevates a space and stands the test of time.

How We Build

A kitchen, taken from concept to grain.

01

Design Consultation

We walk your space, understand how you cook, host, and live — then translate that into a design that fits both your home and your life.

02

Material Selection

Walnut, white oak, ash, quartz, brass — every material is chosen by hand for grain, tone, and how it will age in your light.

What We Build

Millwork for every room worth getting right.

Custom Kitchens

Full kitchen design and fabrication — cabinetry, islands, hoods, and built-in storage tailored to your floor plan.

Millwork & Built-Ins

Bookcases, window seats, mudroom benches, wine walls — architectural millwork woven into the bones of your home.

Custom Closets
