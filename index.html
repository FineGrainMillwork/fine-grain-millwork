Questions

Frequently Asked

How much does custom cabinetry cost?

Full custom kitchens typically run from $15,000 to $40,000+, depending on size, materials, and hardware. Smaller projects like vanities or media walls are priced individually — we'll give you a clear quote after the initial consultation and site measure.

How long does it take?

Timelines depend on project scope, but most full kitchens move from design approval to installation over several weeks in the shop, followed by on-site installation. We'll give you a specific timeline once your design and materials are finalized.

Do you design everything?

Yes — design and manufacturing are handled in-house, from layout and elevations through 3D renderings, so you see the space before we build it.

Do you install?

Yes. Every project is delivered and installed by our own team — not a third-party installer — so anything that needs adjusting on site gets handled by the people who built it.

What areas do you service?

We're based in Barrie, Ontario, and serve the surrounding region. Get in touch with your location and we'll confirm whether it's within our service area.

What warranties do you offer?

We stand behind our workmanship and materials. Ask us during your consultation for the specific warranty terms that apply to your project.
